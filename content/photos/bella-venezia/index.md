Once one of the biggest merchant capitals in the entire world, now the city suffers from overtourism and identity crisis. While walking in the narrow streets, you notice how most of the windows are nailed down and how the paint goes down the wall. It felt like a ghost town.  And the two things that made me feel like it's alive was the sea slowly caressing the pier, and the pasta I had around the corner from the sweetest italian grandpa...
